[ 00 — the problem ]

Windows native IPsec in tunnel mode ignores the Phase 1 crypto you give it. It quietly falls back to DHGroup2, AES128 and SHA1 on every negotiation. You can script around it for days and it won't change. RRAS has its own IKEv2 stack, and that stack does honour a custom policy.

PS C:\>
PS> netsh advfirewall consec show rule name="Primary-Tunnel" verbose
…
MainModeSecMethods:  DHGroup2-AES128-SHA1   # always. regardless.

[ 01 — failure modes ]

Three things
that will break
your tunnel.

01

Native IPsec ignores your Phase 1.

-MainModeCryptoSet doesn't exist on tunnel-mode rules. Custom sets get created but are never linked, and a 2019 → 2022 upgrade doesn't change that.

FIX Switch to RRAS and Set-VpnS2SInterface -CustomPolicy
02

Ghost rules still speak IKEv1.

Native rules left over from earlier attempts keep firing IKEv1 next to your IKEv2. The ASA sees mixed traffic and fills its logs with IKE MM errors.

FIX Get-NetIPsecRule | Remove-NetIPsecRule
03

Wildcard selectors get rejected.

By default RRAS proposes 0.0.0.0/0. An ASA with a specific crypto map answers “Crypto Map Policy not found.”

FIX Explicit New-VpnTrafficSelector (script 03)

[ 02 — the build ]

Six scripts.
One tunnel.

01

setup-rras

Install RemoteAccess and Routing, then configure the server as a LAN router. If you skip the LAN router step, you get error 633.

Install-WindowsFeature RemoteAccess, Routing
02

create-tunnel

Primary and backup S2S interfaces with a full custom policy, authenticated with a PSK read from the environment.

Add-VpnS2SInterface -Protocol Ikev2
03

traffic-selectors

Replace the wildcard 0.0.0.0/0 selectors with exact local and remote ranges. This is the fix for the Cisco "Crypto Map Policy not found" rejection.

New-VpnTrafficSelector
04

verify-tunnel

Read back what RRAS actually negotiates, not just what you configured.

Get-VpnS2SInterface
05

test-connection

Connect the tunnel and probe your application ports end to end.

Test-NetConnection -Port 443
06

cleanup

Tear everything down, including leftover native rules.

Remove-VpnS2SInterface -Force
